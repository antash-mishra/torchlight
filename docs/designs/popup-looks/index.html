Three new looks for Torchlight

Each picture is the real GTK popup, drawn by the app's own code with a different stylesheet, shown empty and then with results for “term”. The rows are sample data. The layout, keys and features stay the same in every direction; only the look changes.

Beam The light follows your selection

Results sit in the dark. The row you are on is lit warm, its neighbours half-lit, the rest fade away, so moving up and down sweeps a torch over your files.

The search icon glows like a lens. Warm charcoal surface, warm white light, a rim lit only near the top.

Motion: the light glides with the selection; when the popup opens, the rows brighten one after another.

Reticle Brackets that lock on

Corner brackets frame the popup instead of a full border, and smaller brackets lock around the selected row.

Query, folders and key hints in Ubuntu Sans Mono; names in Ubuntu Sans. A dashed rule under the query turns solid while you type. One mint signal colour, close to your Mint desktop.

Motion: the frame brackets close in when it opens; the row brackets glide between rows and tighten on Enter.

Monolith Black, white and nothing else

Pure black with a thin white edge, geometric Avant Garde style type (URW Gothic, already installed), and a solid white rule under the query.

The selection is the surface turned inside out: a white bar with black text. Key hints are small white tabs.

Motion: the bar slides between rows and flashes on Enter; nothing else moves.
